## English (en-US, default)

**App name:** Penny Wallet: Expense Tracker

**Short description:** Track spending, budgets and savings. Offline, private, no ads, no account.

**Full description:**

Penny Wallet is a simple expense tracker that keeps your money data on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking: the app doesn't even have internet access.

ADD AN EXPENSE IN SECONDS
Tap +, type the amount, tap a category. Done. Penny learns which category you usually pick and puts it first.

SCAN RECEIPTS
Take a photo of a receipt and Penny reads the total, the shop and the date, right on your phone.

KNOW WHAT'S LEFT
• See what is available to spend across your accounts
• Set a monthly budget and see how much is left per day
• Compare this month with last month at a glance
• Charts by category and month

SAVE FOR GOALS
Set money aside for a new phone or a holiday and watch your progress.

NEVER MISS A BILL
Add rent, subscriptions or your phone plan. Penny reminds you before they're due and can record them for you.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Automatic backups to a folder you pick, optionally password-protected
• Export to CSV
• App lock with fingerprint, face or PIN
• Light and dark theme, your accent colour
• English, German and Italian

Penny Wallet is free. If it helps you, you can leave a tip. It unlocks nothing; everything stays free.
